El Pollo Loco

The chicken are out to get you!

Press SPACE or UP to jump

Press LEFT and RIGHT to move through the level

Press D to throw bottles

Small chicken and chicks can be killed by jumping on them, but the Endboss can only be killed with bottles, so use them sparingly.
