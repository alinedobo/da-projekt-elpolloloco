El Pollo Loco

The chicken are out to get you!

Press SPACE or UP to jump, LEFT and RIGHT to move. Press D to throw bottles.

Chicken and chicks can be killed by jumping on them, but the Endboss can only be killed with bottles, so use them sparingly.
